# 📊 Unemployment Analysis with Python

## 📌 Objective  
To analyze the **unemployment rate in India during the COVID-19 pandemic** using Python.  
The project aims to:  
- Explore patterns and fluctuations in unemployment across states.  
- Understand the socio-economic effects of the lockdown.  
- Provide insights that can aid policymakers and organizations in addressing unemployment challenges.  

---

## 🔎 Steps Performed  
1. **Dataset Preparation**  
   - Loaded `Unemployment in India.csv` dataset (State-wise unemployment trends during COVID-19).  
   - Key columns: `Region`, `Date`, `Frequency`, `Estimated Unemployment Rate (%)`, `Estimated Employed`, `Estimated Labour Participation Rate (%)`, `Area`.  

2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**  
   - Examined statistical summaries of unemployment rates, employment, and participation levels.  
   - Performed **state-wise analysis** to compare pre-lockdown and lockdown unemployment rates.  
   - Visualized data using **heatmaps, bar plots, box plots, scatter plots, and pair plots**.  

3. **Insights Extracted**  
   - Identified states with highest unemployment rates pre- and post-lockdown.  
   - Compared labour participation resilience across states.  
   - Measured correlations between unemployment rate, employment, and labour participation.  

4. **Visualization**  
   - Created meaningful plots to showcase unemployment surges and employment trends.  
   - Highlighted the states most impacted during the pandemic.  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Libraries  
- **Python, Jupyter Notebook**  
- **pandas, numpy** (data handling)  
- **matplotlib, seaborn** (visualization)  

---

## 📈 Key Findings  
- **Sharp rise in unemployment** during the lockdown across multiple states.  
- **Puducherry and Jharkhand** recorded the highest unemployment rates during lockdown.  
- **Haryana and Tripura** had the highest unemployment before the lockdown.  
- States like **Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and West Bengal** consistently struggled with employment numbers both before and during lockdown.  
- **Telangana, Tripura, Meghalaya, and Assam** showed strong labour participation before lockdown; **Meghalaya, Telangana, Tripura, and Andhra Pradesh** led during lockdown.  
- Strong **negative correlation** between unemployment rate and employment levels confirmed.  

---

## 🎯 Outcome  
- Provided **state-wise insights** into unemployment fluctuations due to COVID-19.  
- Showcased how lockdown policies impacted labour markets differently across India.  
- Highlighted correlations between unemployment, employment, and labour participation for better policy understanding.  
- Demonstrated how **data science techniques** can reveal socio-economic impacts of crises and assist in decision-making.
